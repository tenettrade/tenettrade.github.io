HONEST BACKTESTS

Describe a strategy in plain words. Get the real odds.

A deterministic engine runs the test, not an AI guess. You get ranges and sample sizes, and a plain warning when a result looks curve-fitted.

  1. Describe
    Tell the coach your entry, stop and exit rules. It turns them into a strategy draft.
  2. Test
    Win rate and expectancy with likely ranges, tested again on 30% unseen data.
  3. Stress
    Monte Carlo on your next 50 trades shows the losing streaks to expect.
  4. Prop check
    See how it would have handled a challenge's rules at different risk levels.

Backtests use historical data and don't guarantee future results.

Questions

Does Tenet trade for me?

No. It's a coach and a risk supervisor. It places an order only when you ask and confirm it, after the Guardian checks it.

Which brokers and platforms work?

Any broker on MetaTrader 5, on a Windows PC. MT4 and cTrader aren't supported.

Does it give signals?

No. It gives analysis with the evidence and what would prove it wrong, then checks ideas against your own rules. The decision stays yours.

Where does my data go?

Tenet runs on your PC, and your journal and rules stay there. Messages to the coach go to the AI provider you use.
